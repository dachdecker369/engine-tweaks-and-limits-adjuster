## How the work runs

Three levels, as my rules for every project have them:

- **The overview session** (`_OVERVIEW (TweaksV)`) - a fresh one, to which I hand the orchestration after this plan - reads this plan and the findings it names, reads the weekly limits and starts the merge session at my go. It checks the merged main from its diff, asks for my measurement boot, reads it, tags 2.5.0 and pushes; then it plans phase B from the boot's numbers. It is the only session I talk with.
- **The merge session** (`_MERGE (TweaksV)`) makes the scaffold on main first (D5), then turns this plan into one brief per track and starts the seven sessions at my go in its session. It answers what this plan and its decisions answer, sends every decision of mine up to the overview session and brings my answer back down in my words, and sends any finer split it sees up before its go button. While the round runs it alone commits to main and keeps the local notes; it verifies each track from its branch, rebases the tracks onto main in the order of the table, makes the documents one, sets the version, rebuilds from clean, runs the suites and reports the build up.
- **Seven coding sessions**, each in a worktree and on a branch of its own from main at the scaffold's commit, which the app makes, each reporting to the merge session alone. None merges, tags, pushes or sets the version.

Each coding session commits on its branch only, each item its own commit. After every commit it writes its state into its paragraph of the Next steps and into the local notes of its worktree and reports to the merge session in two lines, without stopping; it ends its turn only when its track is done or a decision of mine blocks everything left. No committed file and no commit message names a worktree's folder or a branch: a track's paragraph says "in a worktree and on a branch of its own". Before every push the net diff, every commit's own diff and the messages are searched, as my rules have it.

Every track builds from its findings file in my research tools' folder, `C:\_DEVELOPMENT\_Tools\TweaksV-research\output-1158.16\<topic>_findings_for_the_clean_room_build.txt` - the measurement (section 6), the design (section 8) and the hand-over (the last section: the patterns with their offsets, what each match holds, the facts no pattern protects) - and from the overview session's review beside it, `<topic>_review.md`. No session reads an original's code (D2), and none analyses the dump: a fact the findings lack goes up as a question, or onto the measurement boot's list of things to watch.
