# Roadmap

What I plan for TweaksV next, before it is planned in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) and specified in [SPECIFICATIONS.md](SPECIFICATIONS.md). This file is new with 2.0.0; nothing stood here before 2.1.0, because TweaksV did what I wanted it to do. It held two versions once, 2.2.0 and 2.2.1, both released on 2026-10-25 from a player's report - with NaturalVision Enhanced installed, TweaksV crashed the game; the findings and the design are in NOTES.md and SPECIFICATIONS.md now. Since 2026-10-26 it held six: a limit of the DirectStorage part to lift, not urgent, and five new parts, one adjuster each, towards TweaksV as the one place for tweaking GTA V Enhanced and adjusting its limits; the first of the new parts, Blips, came with 2.3.0 on 2026-10-28 (NOTES.md, Versions), so it holds five now. Each new part is a part like the nine (SPECIFICATIONS.md, section 16): its own file on the shared code - the scanner, the patch module, the relocation core, the three tables - its own section of `TweaksV.ini`, its old plugin in the conflict check, its measurement under `DIAGNOSTIC_MODE`, its suite, its patterns in PATTERNS.md. One minor version per part, a patch version for its fixes; the numbers below are the order I intend, and a version takes the next free number when it ships - a part that is ready before 2.4.0 takes 2.4.0, as Blips took 2.3.0.

How a part is taken over depends on the original's license, as [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md) says of the nine. Code under a license the GPL-3.0 takes in - MIT, BSD, zlib, the GPL-3.0 itself - may be read and adapted: the patterns and the research are credited in CREDITS.md, every line carried over is named in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md, and the part is still built on TweaksV's shared code, as 2.0.0 rebuilt the five plugins, because a plugin merged as it is brings its own scanner, INI and log, what 1.x had. Code without a license, under all rights reserved or under FiveM's terms is idea only: none of it is read, and every game fact behind the part is found anew in the running Enhanced game with my tooling (a test build's dump, Ghidra; PATTERNS.md, "When Game Updates"). The four originals left are Legacy plugins, and no pattern of a Legacy plugin carries over to Enhanced anyway (PATTERNS.md). So every part starts with a research pass in the dump that settles whether the limit is in Enhanced 1.0.1158.16 at all, where it lies, and whether it still binds - Enhanced may have raised it - and the part goes in only when a mod load hits it; a measurement of the limit under `DIAGNOSTIC_MODE`, as the box streamer and the decals have, comes first and says so.

## 2.4.0: a replace-rename inside the game gets POSIX semantics

The DirectStorage part's `UNLOCK_FILES` keeps the game folder's loose files free while the game holds them open, but Windows still refuses the classic rename of a new file over a held one, with access denied, whatever the sharing TweaksV gave the holder - the usual safe way to save a settings file, and how my LuminanceV's panel saved its INI (NOTES.md, T-REPLACE; found 2026-10-26). 2.4.0 lifts that limit for the mods and scripts inside the game's process: the part hooks `NtSetInformationFile` next to `NtCreateFile` and turns a `FileRenameInformation` with ReplaceIfExists whose target lies in the game folder into `FileRenameInformationEx` with `FILE_RENAME_POSIX_SEMANTICS`, on a copy of the caller's buffer, falling back to the call as it was made where the file system refuses the class (a FAT volume, a network share) - one comparison on the information class per call otherwise, nothing refused, nothing outside the game folder touched, the target told the way `NtCreateFile`'s names are; the log and the `UNLOCK_FILES` summary count the renames it turned. The directstorage suite pins the four ways of writing a held file against the stand-in's held file - the classic replace, refused with error 5 with the hook off and going through with it on; the POSIX rename; `ReplaceFile`; the write in place - and README, NOTES (T-REPLACE closed) and the specification's 11.8 follow. Programs outside the game stay as they are: a classic replace from outside still needs the holder to let go, and a POSIX rename works from anywhere.

Not urgent, as I decided on 2026-10-26: the limit is documented, LuminanceV gets a POSIX rename of its own, which also survives any other program holding the file, and no player has reported it. It comes with the next change to the part, or sooner when a mod that is not mine needs it.

## 2.5.0: WeaponLimits - more weapons.meta files and more weapon components

The original: [Weapon Limits Adjuster](https://www.gta5-mods.com/tools/cweaponinfoblob-limit-adjuster) 2.2 by alexguirre, 2018 to 2022-12-19, once named CWeaponInfoBlob Limit Adjuster, game build 2802 the last it supports; the source is at https://github.com/alexguirre/gtav-WeaponLimitsAdjuster. The game has a hard limit on the number of `weapons.meta` files it loads (`CWeaponInfoBlob`, the plugin's INI default 512) and on the weapon components of all `weaponcomponents.meta` files together (`CWeaponComponentInfo`, default 1024, which since its 2.1 also replaces the pool size `gameconfig.xml` gives); past either the game crashes while loading, and add-on weapons are what reaches it. Its license, checked 2026-10-26: the MIT license (2021, alexguirre) for the plugin's own code, the `CWeaponInfoBlob` half, which may be read and adapted with credit; and FiveM's terms for the function that raises the components limit, adapted from FiveM's `PatchWeaponLimits.cpp`, which FiveM's license does not free - its LGPL list names `code/components/citizen-*`, `font-renderer`, `vfs-core` and the client's shared, core, launcher and common folders, not the game components - so that half is idea only. The plugin's files for the conflict check: `WeaponLimitsAdjuster.asi` and the older `CWeaponInfoBlob Adjuster.asi`. Research: both limits in the Enhanced dump - the blob array and the sites that carry its limit (the relocation core's shape, if it is a static array sized in the code), the components pool and the call that reads its size from the configuration (the PackfileLimit part's shape, a redirected call that replaces a count); whether Enhanced's own limits are higher than Legacy's; what a weapon pack needs. Second of the five: add-on weapons are common on Enhanced, and half of the original may be read.

## 2.6.0: SirenSettings - more sirens per vehicle and siren setting IDs past 255

The original: [SirenSetting Limit Adjuster](https://www.gta5-mods.com/scripts/sirensetting-limit-adjuster) 2.0.3 by cp702, 2020 to 2024-07-15, with a 2.0.4 for Legacy build 3788 in its repository, https://github.com/cpast/SirenSetting_Limit_Remover, pushed 2026-05-25. It raises the sirens a vehicle may have from 20 to 32 (`siren1` to `siren32`, `siren_glass` likewise; the 128 bones per vehicle stay), the siren setting ID of `carcols.meta` from 255 to 65535 (0, 255 and 65535 reserved; above 255 the base game's IDs clash with each other) and the number of unique siren settings, and writes a `SirenSettings.log` of every setting and vehicle loaded and every ID clash; its RagePluginHook integration means nothing on Enhanced, which has no RagePluginHook. Its license, checked 2026-10-26: none - the repository has no license file, and its README asks to link to the official upload instead of redistributing - so all rights reserved and idea only, none of its code read. What its author said of the technique on the CitizenFX forum in 2021: the siren setting ID is widened from 8 to 16 bits by turning a good number of byte references in the game's code into word references - many sites, every one to be found anew. Never ported to Enhanced by its author. Research: whether Enhanced keeps the limits of 255 IDs and 20 sirens, where the ID is stored and compared (the vehicle model's variation data, the array of siren settings), and what a car pack with its own `carcols.meta` reaches first; the plugin's file name for the conflict check, probably `SirenSetting_Limit_Adjuster.asi` after its solution. Third: emergency-vehicle packs for Enhanced bring their own siren settings, and 255 IDs run out with many add-ons.

## 2.7.0: PedProps - more props per anchor of a ped

The original: [PedProp Limit Adjuster](https://www.gta5-mods.com/scripts/pedprop-limit-adjuster) 1.0.3889.0 by Zombieguy, uploaded and kept by William Halverd, 2024-04-06 to 2025-07-11: a ped model may have 255 props - hats, glasses and the like - per anchor, an 8-bit limit in RAGE, and the plugin lifts it to 32 bits, mostly for the MP Freemode peds' clothing packs. It is a single-player port of FiveM's pull request 2421 by Disquse, merged 2024-03-19, one file, `code/components/gta-core-five/src/PatchPedPropsLimit.cpp`: a set of the game's functions clamps 32-bit prop indices to 8 bits, and the patch takes the clamps out. Its license, checked 2026-10-26: all rights reserved, so says its page, and FiveM's terms for the pull request's code, which the LGPL list does not free - idea only. Legacy only, from build 2372 on; its author says it is not for Enhanced, and a player reports that it crashes Enhanced. Research: whether Enhanced clamps the prop index the same way and where - the functions the pull request names by their job, found anew; whether anything in story mode reaches 255 props per anchor at all, since the part goes in only when a mod load does; the plugin's file name for the conflict check. Fourth, because story mode rarely needs it.

## 2.8.0: Resources - larger texture and streaming budgets

The original: [Resource Adjuster](https://www.gta5-mods.com/tools/resource-adjuster) 1.0 by zombieguy, 2022-12-21, no longer updated by its author: "a collection of patches", some of which FiveM uses, that raise the VRAM budget for textures and assets - the game's own a little over 1.5 GiB at the highest settings, the plugin's INI about 3.5 GiB - and the memory for streaming the map and props, with the system's memory detected, for PCs with 8 GB of VRAM and more; it does not replace the packfile, heap or siren adjusters. Its license, checked 2026-10-26: none, and no source published - all rights reserved, idea only; a "Resource Adjuster Enhanced" by another uploader appeared on 2025-12-25 without a description or source, was reported as not working and is withdrawn. Research first, because this is the one that may not apply at all: Enhanced runs on DirectX 12 with memory management of its own, and whether it still has the budgets the Legacy game had, where they lie, and whether raising them does anything against the texture loss the plugin was made for is open; a measurement of the budgets and their use under `DIAGNOSTIC_MODE` says so before anything is patched. Last of the five.
